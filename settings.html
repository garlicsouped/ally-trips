SOUND & MUSIC

🎵 Spotify Connected

Once connected, a small floating music icon appears in the top-right corner of every page. Click it, then "Open Player" to launch a separate player window where you search for songs and browse your playlists. Keep that window open in the background and it will keep playing no matter where you click around Ally Trips, since it lives outside the page you're navigating. Requires Spotify Premium.

Good to know: if your browser blocks the player window as a popup, allow popups for this site once and it'll open normally after that.

SPRITE CHARACTERS

These pixel sprites are available when adding a companion to a trip. You can also upload a photo of your friend or just pick any emoji instead, right from the Add Companion screen on a trip.
